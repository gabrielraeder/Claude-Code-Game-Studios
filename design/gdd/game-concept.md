## Core Fantasy

You are a knight dog of the Kennel Kingdom, racing your friends through castle
yards, moats and dungeons: you out-drift them through a gate corner, fire a
catapult stone that banks off a wall into the leader, and steal first place on
the last turn, live on stream, while everyone yells.

---

## Unique Hook

It's like Mario Kart, **and also** it's the DogworksTV channel's own kingdom:
the knight-dog mascot and his breed-knight friends, medieval chaos items
(war horn, catapult, cauldron, falcon, shield), and tracks with living castle
hazards (drawbridges, portcullises, lava vents), made to be streamed with the
channel's community.

### Key Dynamics

- Friends trash-talk and rematch: a close finish makes the group hit Rematch.
- Players learn each track's shortcuts and when a boost pays for an off-road cut.
- Players hold items to defend (shield, a stone behind) or time them for the last lap.
- The back of the pack stays in the race through position-weighted items; the leader defends.

### Core Mechanics

1. Drift and boost driving: 3-tier drift charge, boost pads, surfaces, jumps.
2. Items: position-weighted boxes, five medieval items (more planned), victim-decided hits.
3. Online racing for up to 12, owner-authoritative, with bots.
4. Tracks with shortcuts, elevation, moving hazards and surfaces.
5. Race structure: lobby, countdown, laps, results, rematch (Grand Prix and modes planned).

## Core Loop

### Moment-to-Moment (30 seconds)
Steer, drift through a corner to charge blue → orange → purple sparks, release
for a boost, grab an item box, fire or hold the item, dodge a hazard.

### Short-Term (5-15 minutes)
A 3-lap race: fight for position, learn a shortcut, finish, see the results, rematch.

### Session-Level (30-120 minutes)
A run of rematches on the group's favourite tracks, or a Grand Prix cup across
4 tracks with points (planned).

### Long-Term Progression
Mastery (lines, shortcuts, 200cc), cups and cc classes, time trials and ghosts,
kart customization (planned). No grind-based unlock economy is planned.

### Retention Hooks
- **Curiosity**: new tracks and cups, new items, modes (team racing, elimination race).
- **Investment**: your racer and customised kart.
- **Social**: friends and the stream community racing together.
- **Mastery**: drift lines, shortcuts, time-trial ghosts, 200cc.

## Game Pillars

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-21.

### Pillar 1: Online with friends first
Everything works for 12 racers online and reads clearly on stream; a feature
that does not sync cleanly is redesigned, not shipped offline-only.

*Design test*: a hazard driven by shared kart-pushed physics objects vs one that
is a pure function of the synced race clock → the clock-driven one.

### Pillar 2: Drift is the skill
Drifting, racing lines and shortcuts decide the race; items keep the race close
but always leave a chance to react.

*Design test*: an item that wipes out the leader with no counterplay vs one with
a dodge or shield window → the one with counterplay.

### Pillar 3: Mario Kart-grade quality
Nothing "simple just to be playable soon"; a feature goes in when it feels and
looks at the level of the genre's best.

*Design test*: shipping a rough version now vs holding it until it plays and
looks right → hold it. (Owner, 2026-11-21: "Não podemos fazer algo simples aqui
só para termos algo jogavel breve.")

### Pillar 4: The Kennel Kingdom
Knight dogs, storybook medieval, the DogworksTV identity; everything original,
no Nintendo IP.

*Design test*: a generic racing element vs a medieval-dog version of it → the
medieval-dog version; anything recognisable from another game's characters,
items or tracks → redesign it.

### Anti-Pillars (What This Game Is NOT)

- **NOT a simulator**: arcade handling; realism never beats fun or readability.
- **NOT open world**: races on tracks; an adventure mode comes only after everything else.
- **NOT boats or planes**: carts and war-bikes on land, with anti-gravity and wall-riding sections.
- **NOT strangers or monetization**: no matchmaking with strangers, no store, no live-service economy.

### What exists in code (2026-11-21)

- **Driving** (`src/vehicle/kart_sim.gd`): planar kinematic kart with a height layer; 3-tier drift (cart tiers 0.9/1.9/3.0 s, bike 0.7/1.5/2.4 s), boost pads, ramps, surfaces (caps grass .55, sand .50, mud .40, rubble .60, shallows .75; slick grip .35 with a 0.6 s tail), hit profiles, respawn. Stats in `assets/data/vehicles/` (cart max 26.0 m/s accel 14.0; bike 24.96 / 18.2).
- **Track model** (`src/test_track/track_model.gd`): spline roads with heights and banks, branches, shortcuts, a crossover, surface zones and wall openings; generated by `tools/gen_*.py`.
- **Items** (`src/items/`): War Horn, Catapult Stone, Cauldron Spill, Falcon, Shield of the Realm; position-weighted; hits decided by the victim's PC.
- **Bots** (`src/ai/`): personalities (e.g. Hothead, Careful, Shortcut Hunter), racing lines, items, hazards and zones.
- **Online** (`src/net/`): host/join by IP, version handshake, owner-authoritative karts, interpolated remote karts.
- **Hazards** (`src/track/`): drawbridge, barrels, portcullis, lava vents, ore cart, as pure functions of the synced race time.
- **Race flow, HUD, audio, input**: lobby, countdown, laps, results, rematch; stream HUD and minimap; rebinding.

## Risks and Open Questions

### Design Risks
- Items vs skill balance at 12 racers (Pillar 2): position-weighted odds tuned for 12 but not yet played by a full group.
- Feel of hazards, surfaces and elevation has had little human play (stories 008 and 009 feel sign-offs pending play).

### Technical Risks
- The mesh-collision physics rewrite (022) touches every system that reads the track (bots, items, hazards, net, camera): the largest risk in the plan.
- Online sync of physics on meshes for 12 owner-authoritative karts, including anti-gravity.
- Godot 4.8 is a beta (beta3); APIs may change before stable.
- Blender → Godot import of rigged, animated characters and collision-tagged tracks is unproven here.

### Market Risks
- None assessed: the game is for the channel and friends, not sold (not researched).

### Scope Risks
- The approved roadmap is large (Phase A foundation, then ~15 features and modes); one person plays and signs off.
- Existing stories 010 and 012-020 must be replanned on the new base.

### Open Questions

- How many cups and tracks for the full vision? (Mario Kart-style cups of 4 tracks assumed; count `INTENT UNKNOWN`.)
- Online for modes beyond racing (team racing, elimination): same 12-player model? To be settled in their GDDs.
