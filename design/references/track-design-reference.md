# Track Design Reference: What Makes Kart Tracks Feel Rich

> Research reference for story 003 (Castle Circuit, Dungeon Depths). Researched 2026-11-19.
> We study **patterns and numbers only**. No layout, landmark or look below may be copied.
> Our tracks stay original (see the brief's "No Nintendo IP" rule, `design/game-brief.md`).
> **(est.)** marks an estimate: read from a map image, derived from a time, or a judgment call.
> All other facts are cited. Course maps were viewed at the image URLs given; none are stored in the repo.

## 1. Sources and how lap times were derived

- MK8 Deluxe 150cc world records (3 laps, Time Trial with 3 mushrooms): https://mkwrs.com/mk8dx/ (checked 2026-11-19).
  Expert lap = WR / 3. An ordinary good player is roughly 15-30% slower **(est.)**.
- CTR relic times (3 laps; Sapphire = easy target, Platinum = expert) from the Crash Bandicoot wiki page source:
  https://crashbandicoot.fandom.com/wiki/Cortex_Castle_(track), https://crashbandicoot.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Mines, https://crashbandicoot.fandom.com/wiki/Crash_Cove
- Course layouts: Super Mario Wiki course pages (URLs per track below). SASRT: Sonic wiki pages. DKR: https://www.mariowiki.com/Darkmoon_Caverns
- We could not find published lap lengths in meters for any of these games, so all comparisons are in **seconds**, then converted with our kart speeds.

## 2. Eight reference tracks

### 2.1 Mario Kart Stadium (MK8DX), the early, simple track
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_Stadium. Map: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/3/39/MK8DX_Mario_Kart_Stadium_Map.png. Overview: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/1/1d/MK8-FirstCircuit-Overview.png
- **Time:** WR 1:33.6, so about **31 s/lap** for an expert and 36-40 s for a normal player **(est.)**. This is the same lap time as our current Castle Circuit. The difference is density, not length.
- **Corners (est., from map):** about 8. That is 2 hairpins (a tight U at the bottom-right and the raised hairpin at the top-left), 3 sweepers on the long diagonal, and 3 near-90° bends.
- **Width:** at one right bend the road splits into **3 parallel lanes** (an inside apex lane, a middle lane and an outer lane with dash panels). Elsewhere it is one wide road.
- **Shortcuts:** **5 small cuts**: the first turn, between the pipes, a ramp to an item box, a grass patch and a sand patch. Each needs a mushroom **or**, without an item, a skilled mini-turbo hop.
- **Elevation:** 1 anti-gravity strip, the raised hairpin and 1 glide section.
- **Item rows:** at least 2. One can be skipped with a ramp. Dash panels sit on the outer lane and 3 on the final straight.
- **Scenery:** an enclosed stadium with stands holding about 20,000 sprite spectators, ad banners, fireworks and a city skyline. The overview shows the track snaking up in front of tall stands: the scenery is a full ring of walls at stand height.

### 2.2 Bowser's Castle (MK8DX), the castle track
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Bowser%27s_Castle_(Mario_Kart_8). Map: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/e/ef/MK8DX_Bowser%27s_Castle_Map.png. Start: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/0/03/MK8_Bowser%27s_Castle_Starting_Line.png
- **Time:** WR 1:57.1, so about **39 s/lap**.
- **Sections:** castle gate start, left-right, anti-gravity hall, a **square U-turn**, a **fork** at a giant statue, downhill curves into a lava cavern, a glide out of the castle, a slight left, a U-turn and a **final split** (one branch has a ramp).
- **Corners (est., from map):** about 11. That is 2 U-turns, about 5 hard 90° "square" corners, 3 S-curves on the twisting descent and 1 kink. The map is visibly built from **right angles**: castle architecture reads as square corners.
- **Splits:** 2 forks. **Shortcut:** 1, a mushroom cut through grass before the finish.
- **Hazards:** 6 kinds: statue lasers on laps 2-3 only, fire bars, a spinner, cannon boulders, lava geysers and a punching statue that sends ripples across the road.
- **Scenery:** the start is a gate in a tall towered wall with flame jets and banners. Patrolling minions, airships outside, a foundry interior with molten-iron lava.

### 2.3 Wario's Gold Mine (MKWii / MK8DX), the mine with moving carts
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Wario%27s_Gold_Mine. Maps: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/5/5d/Wario_Gold_Mine_MKWii_layout.png, https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/7/71/MK8DX_Wii_Wario%27s_Gold_Mine_Map.png. Screenshots: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/2/21/MKW_Wario%27s_Gold_Mine_Course_Overview.png, https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/2/2d/MK8-DLC-Course-Wii_Wario%27sGoldMine-screenshot01.jpg
- **Time:** WR (MK8DX) 2:00.6, so about **40 s/lap**.
- **Sections (9):** plank start, dip, U-turn, bat tunnel, cavern, second dip, mine-cart hall, short dip, outside final stretch with a 90° right to the finish.
- **Corners (est., from map):** about 12. That is mostly sweepers and S-wiggles along the outer edge, 1 U-turn and one 90° at the finish. The overall loop is a lumpy rectangle.
- **Width:** it **narrows to planks with rails** at the start and **widens** in the cart hall. Few guard rails and a "long drop off the edge" (open fall-offs).
- **Alternate route:** follow the cart rail into a **narrower path with 2 dash panels** and a ramp that rejoins at the final straight.
- **Hazards:** carts on a rail that weaves left, middle and right across the wide hall, moving the same way as racers (Wii). Swoop swarms in the tunnel. Exhaust pipes that block vision.
- **Item sets:** about 6 on Wii (trough, half-pipe, ramp, carts, after the right turn, finish half-pipe).
- **Scenery:** cliff-side houses on stilts, pipes, timber trestles, a hanging portrait banner over the cart hall. The screenshot shows a dense vertical town on both sides and rails visible far ahead (a vista).

### 2.4 Mushroom Gorge (MKWii / MK8DX), the track with notable shortcuts
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Mushroom_Gorge. Maps: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/b/b9/Mushroom_Gorge_MKWii_layout.png, https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/3/3a/MK8DX_Wii_Mushroom_Gorge_Map.png
- **Time:** WR 1:26.5, so about **29 s/lap**: short, but nearly half the lap is choice-heavy bounce sections.
- **Corners (est.):** about 8, with 1 U-turn around an abyss at the end.
- **Splits: 2 forks per lap.** One is long and safe versus short over a gap. The other is a high route with **2 item sets** versus a low, slightly faster route with **1 item pair**: the trade is items against time.
- **Shortcuts: 2.** One crosses an abyss at the first item row and needs a mushroom or a skilled hop. The other is a final-turn gap jump. **Hazards:** 3 walkers on the final U-turn, plus open pits.

### 2.5 Mount Wario (MK8DX), heavy elevation
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Mount_Wario. Map: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/5/5b/MK8DX_Mount_Wario_Map.png
- Point-to-point in **3 sections**, WR 1:39.8. **4 distinct zones**: summit and ice, cave and dam, forest, ski slope.
- Map: a slalom of about 6 S-wiggles plus a wide curved dam band. Turn types are icy downhill turns, two 90° turns in the dam, a slalom and moguls.
- **5 shortcuts**, 3 of them mushroom-only. A **3-way forest split**, ramps, glide ramps and dash panels in streams.
- Lesson for us: **zone changes every ~30 s** keep a long course fresh. Not applicable now (we are a flat loop).

### 2.6 Rainbow Road (MK8DX), the complex late track
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Rainbow_Road_(Mario_Kart_8). Map: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/5/5e/MK8DX_Rainbow_Road_Map.png
- WR 1:57.9, so about **39 s/lap**. Two space stations, **three figure-8 twists**, conveyor belts that reverse every 50 s, a cannon glide through 3 rings, then **two intertwined paths** where falling off the upper one lands you on the lower one.
- The map shows **a separate chunk** (station 2) disconnected by a glide, and a wide **180° turn with dash panels on the outside edge**.
- Lesson: late tracks layer choices on top of turns (path over path, risky edge lines).

### 2.7 Peach Gardens (MKDS / MK8DX), a castle garden with a maze split
Source: https://www.mariowiki.com/Peach_Gardens_(race_course). Map: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/1/12/MK8DX_DS_Peach_Gardens_Map.png. Bird's-eye: https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/c/cd/MK8D_Peach_Gardens_Birds_Eye.png
- **Corners (est., from map):** about 10, almost all **square 90° corners** around a rectangular loop, plus 1 circular plaza with central islands.
- **Width:** a **grid of square hedge blocks** splits the road into about 6 lanes for one section ("maze" parterres), then rejoins. The circular section has 3 high hedges that briefly split the road.
- **Shortcuts:** 3 grass or flower cuts (boost needed). **Hazards:** roaming chained ball-beasts, moles at corners. The only unfenced edge is the pond.
- **Scenery (bird's-eye):** hedges about 1 m tall line nearly 100% of the road, rows of cone trees every few meters, a **white crenellated curtain wall with round towers** enclosing the whole garden (a backdrop, never driven), a big central topiary statue, rose arches over the road and a fenced dirt start straight.

### 2.8 Crash Team Racing (Cortex Castle, Dragon Mines) and SASRT (Adder's Lair, Dragon Canyon)
- **Cortex Castle** (https://crashbandicoot.fandom.com/wiki/Cortex_Castle_(track)). Relic 1:32 to 2:35, so **31-52 s/lap**. Prima guide: "a lot of right-angle turns, some big jumps, and ledges without guardrails".
  The wiki's turn-by-turn description gives about **15 corners (est.)**: 2 U-turns, about 10 sharp or 90°, 3 gentle.
  It also has 4 boost pads, **5 drop-down spiders** in corridors, 1 fork, 1 shortcut (a side room that cuts a corner, then a big drop), wooden stairs, a hedge section and a drawbridge at the castle entrance. The lap alternates **inside and outside** the castle 4 times.
- **Dragon Mines** (https://crashbandicoot.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Mines). Relic 0:54 to 1:28, so **18-29 s/lap**: tiny. Prima: "not a whole lot of room ... quite dark in places".
  It has about 6 corners plus 1 climbing spiral, 2 mine-cart crossings, an underground lake and 1 boost pad. **2 shortcuts:** follow a cart rail off-road (the hardest in the game, carts run you over), and a rough-ground cut on the last bend.
- **Crash Cove** (CTR's first track). Relic 0:52 to 1:17 (17-26 s/lap). "always curves gradually to the left, and there are no track hazards". Crates come in **rows of four**. 1 underwater-ramp shortcut.
- **SASRT Adder's Lair** (https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Adder%27s_Lair). A medieval-fantasy valley with ruins, a statue and a volcano. Parts of the track **are destroyed every lap, changing the layout**.
  **Dragon Canyon** (https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Canyon): on lap 2 debris blocks the road and forces a river detour; on lap 3 the bridge to the signature hairpin is destroyed.
- **DKR Darkmoon Caverns** (https://www.mariowiki.com/Darkmoon_Caverns). Time-trial ghost 2:12-2:20, about **45 s/lap**. Tunnels lit by blinking arrows, an S-tunnel, a corkscrew, 2 loops, craters, lasers. The tunnels are used as **pacing punctuation** between open areas.

| Corners per lap | 6-8 | 10-15 | map counts **(est.)** |
| Hairpins / U-turns per lap | 1-2 | 2-3 | maps, Cortex Castle text |
| Zones / sections per lap | 3-4 | 5-9 | Gold Mine 9, Bowser's Castle 7 |

| Boost pads per lap | 2-4 | 4-8 | Cortex Castle 4, Stadium ~5 **(est.)** |

| Major landmarks per lap | 2-3 | 4-6 | one per zone **(est.)** |

**Key reading:** the first track of a top kart racer is about as long in seconds as our current Castle Circuit. "Rich" comes from **density per second**, which our lap lacks:
- about one corner every **3-4 s**
- a choice (fork, lane split or shortcut) every **8-12 s**
- a new zone every **8-15 s**
- an enclosed, landmark-heavy backdrop

Our lap has one corner every ~5 s, 2 choices and an open field **(est.)**.

## 4. Patterns that create complexity without unfairness

1. **Rhythm: fast, technical, fast.** Cluster 2-4 corners into a technical section, then release onto a straight of **2-6 s** for overtaking and item use. Never two long straights back-to-back. Never more than ~7 s without a steering input **(est.)**.
2. **Corner variety in every lap.** Mix hairpins (drift-charge payoff), 90° squares (architecture), S-bends and chicanes (weight transfer), sweepers (flat-out nerve) and kinks (line choice).
   Use both directions, roughly 55/45 **(est.)**. Crash Cove's "always left" is a beginner-only pattern.
3. **Width as pacing.** Pinch the road (gate, bridge, plank path) right **after** a wide section so packs funnel and items land. Widen it before hairpins so drifters and safe drivers both fit.
   Gold Mine (planks, then a wide hall) and Bowser's Castle (gate, then hall) both do this.
4. **Splits are trades, not puzzles.** Every fork trades one currency for another:
   - shorter but hazardous (Gorge's gap route)
   - more items but slower (Gorge's high route)
   - dash panels but a narrow line (Gold Mine's cart path)
   - the outside lane with boosts versus the inside apex (Stadium's 3 lanes)

   Both sides must rejoin within ~5-10 s and be visible from the fork.
5. **Tiered shortcut access.** The best MK shortcuts are open to **an item OR a skill** (a mushroom, or a charged drift hop). One shortcut per lap half. Put the hardest one late in the lap, where it decides races (the brief: "laps 2-3 shortcuts decide the winner").
6. **Telegraph everything.** A statue that points the way at a turn (Bowser's Castle). Carts that move the same way as racers. Lap-gated hazards (lasers on laps 2-3 only). Dark tracks are criticized for readability (Dragon Mines' "quite dark in places"), so **light the outside of every corner**.
7. **Inside and outside alternation.** Cortex Castle crosses its wall 4 times per lap, and Darkmoon Caverns punctuates open areas with tunnels. Each crossing is a free "zone change" and a framing arch.
8. **Vistas and a skyline anchor.** One tall landmark visible from most of the lap (the stadium spire, the castle towers ringing Peach Gardens). Glimpses of track you will drive later (the Gold Mine trestles, the Stadium hairpin seen from the start).
9. **Enclosure.** In every studied screenshot the road is framed on both sides within one road-width (stands, hedges, house walls, cliffs). Open edges are the exception and are used deliberately as risk (Gold Mine's drops, the Peach Gardens pond).
10. **Lap evolution (later).** SASRT changes the route per lap (debris on lap 2, a broken bridge on lap 3), and MK8 turns hazards on for laps 2-3. This is cheap novelty from the same geometry.

## 5. Converting to our kart

- Our speeds: 26 m/s top, ~35 m/s boosting. The current Castle Circuit is 856 m in ~31 s, which means near-flat-out the whole lap.
- With 12-15 corners, a realistic **average speed is 20-23 m/s (est.)**. So:

| Target | Castle Circuit | Dungeon Depths |
|---|---|---|
| Lap length | **1,000-1,150 m** | **950-1,100 m** |
| Lap time, good player | **45-50 s** | **48-54 s** (tighter, darker) |
| Race, 3 laps | ~2:20-2:30 | ~2:25-2:40 |
| Corners | 13-15 | 15-18 |
| Zones | 5 | 5 |
| Item rows | 3 (first at ~250 m, so the first item volley lands ~30 s in, matching the brief) | 3 |
| Boost pads | 4-6 | 4-6 |

- **Corner radii (est.; tune with `tools/track_check.gd` and play):**
  - hairpin: centerline radius 9-14 m (exit about 12-16 m/s, needs a drift)
  - 90°: 16-25 m
  - S or chicane: 2 opposite arcs of 20-30 m with 10-20 m between them
  - sweeper: 45-90 m (flat out with a light drift)
  - kink: over 120 m
- **Widths:** the main road is currently 18 m wide everywhere (`road_half_width = 9.0`) and branches are 4.4-5.2 m. Recommended:
  - grid and start straight: 18-20 m (12-slot grid)
  - standard road: 12-14 m
  - pinches (gate, bridge, village lane): 8-10 m
  - plazas and caverns: 20-26 m
  - shortcuts: 4.5-6 m

  **This needs per-point width on the centerline.** Today width is one constant, so that is an engine-side change to flag.
- **Straights:** the longest is 130-180 m (5-7 s). Typical straights are 40-90 m. Corner-to-corner gaps under 25 m only inside chicanes.

## 6. Castle Circuit: original guidance (flat)

A storybook castle seen as a **day out at the tourney**. The lap goes from the tourney field, through the gate, through the village and around the keep, then out along the walls. All shapes below are ours; the order and themes are suggestions to iterate on paper first.

| # | Zone (approx. length) | Road and corners | Width | Set pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tourney Field**, start (~200 m) | Grid straight, then a fast right sweeper around the jousting lists, then a kink | 18-20 m, narrowing to 14 m | Grandstands with a crowd, striped pavilion tents, jousting fence along the inside, pennant strings |
| 2 | **Moat and Gatehouse** (~180 m) | Long left sweeper hugging the moat edge (open water edge, low curb), then a 90° right into the **gatehouse arch** | 14 m, pinching to **9 m** at the arch | Moat water plane, curtain wall with towers, gatehouse with portcullis teeth. **Drawbridge shortcut** (below) |
| 3 | **Market Village** (~260 m) | 90° left, then an **S-bend around the market well**, a **lane split around a central row of stalls** (2 lanes, ~8 m each, rejoin after ~70 m), then a 90° right | 10-12 m streets, 2x8 m at the split | Timber-frame house facades on both sides (enclosure), stalls with striped awnings, well, barrels and crates, hanging signs, washing lines |
| 4 | **Keep Courtyard** (~200 m) | Wide plaza, then a **hairpin left around the keep's statue fountain**, then a chicane through the inner gate | 22-24 m plaza, 10 m chicane | The keep (tallest landmark, visible from most of the lap), statue of a knight dog, banners, training dummies. **Secret passage** entrance in the plaza's outer wall |
| 5 | **Rampart Gardens**, home stretch (~250 m) | Back gate, a long right sweeper along the outer wall through an orchard, a short straight, a **second hairpin (right)** onto the start straight | 14 m, then 16 m | Orchard trees, hedges, trebuchet on the lawn (vista prop), windmill on a distant hill, then the stands again |

Corner total: 2 hairpins, 4 x 90°, 2 S/chicanes, 3 sweepers and 2 kinks, so **13** (est. 1,090 m). Directions: 6 left, 7 right.

**Item rows:** row 1 at the end of the tourney straight (~250 m), row 2 at the village split (one row of 2 in each lane), row 3 in the keep plaza before the hairpin.

**Boost pads:** on the outside exit of each hairpin, at the drawbridge landing and at the passage exit.

**Shortcuts:**
- **Drawbridge (mid-lap 1st half).** The main road sweeps the long way around the moat. A **5 m wide plank bridge with no rails** cuts straight across the moat to the gate. It saves ~1.5-2 s **(est.)** but must be entered from a tight angle that needs a drift-boost or a careful line. Missing it means a moat dunk and a respawn.
  *Later:* the drawbridge raises and lowers on a visible timer (moving hazard story), or is raised on lap 3 (lap evolution).
- **Secret passage (2nd half, the decider).** A dark 4.5 m corridor behind a hanging tapestry in the plaza wall. It runs through the cellar and exits past the hairpin, saving ~2 s **(est.)**. It has two 90° bends inside, so only a well-held drift line keeps the gain. Torches inside and a glimpse of light at the exit telegraph it.
  *Later:* a stair drop at the exit (elevation).
- **Optional boost-only cuts (1-2):** the village green corner and the orchard lawn on the last hairpin. They lose time without a boost.

**Later stories add:**
- elevation: a rampart run on top of the walls, a raised keep bridge, a dip under the gatehouse
- moving hazards: drawbridge timing, catapult stones landing in the plaza (telegraphed by shadows), a jousting knight crossing the field on lap 2-3, a swinging tavern sign

## 7. Dungeon Depths: original guidance (flat)

The castle's **underworld**: wine cellars, then torch galleries, a great cavern, mine works and a lava forge. Light is wayfinding: warm torches mark the road edge, lava glow marks danger, and cold blue crystal marks the shortcuts.

| # | Zone (approx. length) | Road and corners | Width | Set pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cellar Vaults**, start (~170 m) | Grid under vaulted arches, a 90° right, then a chicane between barrel racks | 18 m, then 12 m | Stone arches every ~12 m, giant casks, racks of barrels, lanterns |
| 2 | **Torch Gallery** (~200 m) | Winding S-tunnel (3 alternating bends), then a **pillar hall**: the road splits around 2 rows of columns (3 lanes, rejoin after ~60 m) | 9-10 m tunnel, 3x7 m lanes | Torches every ~12 m on both walls, carved pillars, chained gates, skull niches |
| 3 | **Great Cavern** (~230 m) | Opens wide. A long left sweeper along a **lava-lake shore** (open edge, lava = respawn), then a **hairpin around a huge stalagmite** | 22-26 m | Stalagmites, glowing crystal clusters, the lava lake, a giant fossil-like rock arch (vista), a waterfall of lava in the background |
| 4 | **Mine Works** (~220 m) | Main road goes 90° right, a hairpin around the ore crusher, then 90° left. The **mine-cart rail shortcut** runs straight through | 12 m main | Timber supports every ~8 m, ore carts, pulley wheel, scaffolds, lanterns on posts, ore piles |
| 5 | **Lava Forge**, home (~200 m) | Bridge over the lava channel, an S-bend through anvils and furnaces, then a long 90° onto the grid | 10 m bridge, then 14 m | Forge furnaces (orange glow), anvils, bellows, chains, cooling racks. **Lava-gap** shortcut |

Corner total: 3 hairpins, 4 x 90°, 3 S/chicanes, 3 sweepers and 2 kinks, so **15** (est. 1,020 m). This is darker and narrower than Castle Circuit, so keep the walls bright at corners.

**Item rows:** in the pillar hall (one per lane), on the cavern sweeper and on the forge straight.

**Shortcuts:**
- **Mine-cart rail (mid-lap).** A 4.5 m wooden-sleeper path follows the rail tunnel, straight where the main road zig-zags. It saves ~2 s **(est.)**. Its cost now (flat) is rough sleepers that slow you unless you enter boosting.
  *Later:* carts on the rail (moving hazard). Keep them moving the same way as racers and visible from the entrance.
- **Lava gap (late lap, the decider).** A ~6-8 m strip of lava cuts a forge corner. Flat now, so it is crossable only above a speed threshold (e.g. near boost speed **(est.)**). Below it you sink and respawn.
  *Later:* a real ramp jump over the gap (elevation).
- **Optional:** the inside lane of the pillar hall is shortest but has the narrowest gaps.

**Later stories add:**
- elevation: a descending spiral entry from the castle, a climb out of the forge, a stalactite drop
- moving hazards: mine carts, lava geysers on a rhythm (telegraphed by bubbling), falling stalactites with shadow warnings, a lap-3 bridge collapse that reroutes onto the cart rail (lap evolution)

## 8. Scenery props to build (simple low-poly primitives)

Densities are per 100 m of road edge **(est.)**. Use instanced meshes for repeats so 12 racers still run smoothly. A rule from the reference screenshots: something stands within **one road-width of each edge on 70-90% of the lap**.

**Real-time lights:** only a few. Fake most torches with emissive material and keep real lights to ~6-10 per lap in the dungeon **(est.)**. Bake or fake the rest.
