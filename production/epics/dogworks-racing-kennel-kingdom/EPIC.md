# Epic: Dogworks Racing: Kennel Kingdom

> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md`
> **Engine**: Godot 4.8-beta3 (GDScript)

## Goal

Knight dogs race armored carts and war-bikes through a storybook castle kingdom: drift to charge boosts, hurl catapult stones at your friends, and cross the finish line in 12-racer online chaos.

## Scope

The brief's 7 Features, built in its Build order: online core and driving first, then tracks, items, bots, racers, and race flow.
