## Tolerances (set by this story)

- Height: |mesh - spline| <= 0.050 m on every held sample. The bake puts every deck vertex exactly on the frozen surface, so the only error is the planar triangle between vertices <= 1.0 m apart against the frozen bank and corner fans; 5 cm is under a tenth of the kart's suspension travel and far under the 3 m level separation.
- Surface id: must match on every held sample, i.e. every sample farther than 0.25 m from a zone, pad or ramp outline. The bake clips deck triangles exactly to zone and pad outlines, so only the cross-section interpolation (lerped mitred normals vs the frozen perpendicular projection) and the ramp wedge cells can move an outline; 0.25 m is a quarter of a kart width.
- Wall offset: |mesh - spline| <= 0.050 m on every held sample. The mesh wall vertices sit on the frozen offset; the chord between mitred vertices <= 0.3 m apart deviates by w(1 - cos) of the half turn per sample.
- Wall probe: a 0.01 m sphere. It measures the wall offset along the sweep ray, not kart contact: a 0.1 m sphere touches a wall that runs nearly along the ray (a steep taper) with its side, up to r / sin(angle) early (about 0.32 m at 17 degrees on the drawbridge), which is the probe, not the mesh.
- Not held (own rows): samples within 0.25 m of a frozen height step (ramp edges; a deck edge over an open drop), the drawbridge deck (a rigid mover box in the mesh by design, a road-following deck in the frozen game), wall openings (ADR-0002: an opening is the absence of wall) and walls inside another road's corridor (the frozen query lets the kart change road there, so that wall is not a barrier).
